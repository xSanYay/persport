I started with HTML when I was in 5th. Bootstrap 2 was everywhere, <marquee> still looked cool, and I mostly built random pages because making something appear on a screen felt amazing.

By 8th, I had moved on to Android. I was rooting phones, editing APKs to get free gems, messing with system files, and eventually built my first app using one of those block-based app builders. Proper tutorials were not exactly easy to find then, especially when 100 MB of internet cost around ₹45, so most of it was just me trying things and hoping I did not break the phone.

In 2016, I started a YouTube channel where I mixed songs with videos. One of them reached 8 million views. I stopped because of my 10th boards, but I still remember watching it compete with Sunflower, which is funny because I still love that song.

Later, I spent an unnecessary amount of time deciding between C++ and Python. I finally picked Python because building a GUI looked easier. That decision took me into OpenCV, face detection, ArUco markers, robotics, Astra Robotics, and IEEE RAS.

Today, I work as a backend engineer at HappyFox, mostly building AI products and the systems around them. I am also working on ProtoDeccan, not because I have everything figured out, but because I wanted to learn by building something of my own. And yeah, I spend probably too much time thinking about financial markets, businesses, incentives, and why almost every confident prediction eventually goes wrong.
